ABOUT / XINYUN ZHANG

XINYUN ZHANG

VISUAL / SOUND / SPACE

Xinyun Zhang is a media artist working at the intersection of sound, technology, visual media, and spatial experience. Her practice explores the translation between different forms of media and perception, creating situations in which sound, image, movement, and space interact and transform one another.

Her works take forms ranging from interactive installations and audiovisual environments to experimental instruments and live performance. Technology often functions as a connective structure within these works, allowing sound, image, physical objects, performers, and audiences to influence one another in real time.

Zhang studied Digital Media Art at the Central Academy of Fine Arts in Beijing, developing a multidisciplinary practice across design, moving image, interaction, and spatial media. During an exchange in Media Art at the Karlsruhe University of Arts and Design (HfG Karlsruhe), her engagement with the Sound program brought sound and experimental electronic practices into increasing focus. She is currently studying Klang & Realität at the Robert Schumann Hochschule Düsseldorf.

LIVE AUDIOVISUAL PERFORMANCE · 2026
OVERLAP
WATCH ON YOUTUBE

Overlap is a collaborative audiovisual performance by Xinyun Zhang and Yunfei Zhang, based on Circuitry-Based Sound. Each performer operates an independent electronic circuit, manipulating its parameters to generate sound and visual signals in real time. Through continuous listening and mutual response, the performers establish a dynamic dialogue between their two systems.

Their individual actions intertwine, giving rise to an ever-evolving audiovisual composition. What overlaps is not only the signals themselves, but also the boundaries between performer and instrument, action and response, control and being controlled.

MOO(?)

WHO OWNS AN EMOTION?

Prolonged behavioural conditioning and environmental intervention alter gene expression, leaving visible traces in the cows' coat patterns. Each pattern records a distinct emotional state, translating behavioural experience into biological form.

ABOUT MOO(?)

Moo(?) is a speculative design project that imagines a system in which dairy cows' emotional states can be conditioned, biologically encoded, and transformed into commercial products.

Through a collection of 17 emotional milk varieties, the project examines how biotechnology and design might extend human control over animal life. As emotions become measurable, reproducible, and marketable, the boundary between living beings and consumer goods begins to dissolve.

EIN TRAUM VON JERUSALEM

Performances

10 October 2026 — STF Düsseldorf

FULL PERFORMANCE

PERFORMANCE VIDEOCOMING SOON

Ein Traum von Jerusalem explores the desire to remain in a dream and the inevitability of waking up.

Through live vocal processing, sound, dynamic light, and projected architecture, an imagined Jerusalem takes shape as a place of memory and longing. Architectural forms emerge, dissolve, and reappear, creating a world that feels almost tangible but never quite within reach.

Repeated calls to awaken interrupt the dream, drawing the performance into a struggle between the comfort of an idealized world and the reality of having to leave it behind.
